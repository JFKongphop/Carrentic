CARRENTIC — DEMO CLICKS (4 pages)
=================================

Start: open  http://localhost:3001


Step 1  →  Landing  (/)
  Click  "Enter the fleet"


Step 2  →  Tokenize  (/issue)
  Click  "Tokenize a car"  (left sidebar)
  Click  the issue button
  Wait for it to finish, click the HashScan link


Step 3  →  Fleet  (/fleet)
  Click  "Fleet"  (left sidebar)
  Show the car, the value, and the owners


Step 4  →  Owners & rent  (/accounts)
  Click  "Owners & rent"  (left sidebar)
  Show who owns shares and their rent

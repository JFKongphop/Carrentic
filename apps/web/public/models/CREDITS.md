# 3D car model credits

The car models in this folder (`*.gltf`) were obtained from the open-source
project **Custom-Cars-3D** by Viktor Velizarov:
https://github.com/ViktorVelizarov/Custom-Cars-3D

That repository does not carry an explicit license, and the individual car
models were most likely sourced from Sketchfab, where many are published under
**CC-BY** (free to use *with attribution*) or similar terms.

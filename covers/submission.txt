1. NAME + TAGLINE
==============================================================

Carrentic — Own the car. Earn its rent.


==============================================================
2. SHORT DESCRIPTION  (the hook — max 100 characters)
==============================================================

Own a share of a real car as an ERC-3643 token on Hedera, and earn its rent on-chain.


==============================================================
3. DESCRIPTION  (the full "what it does")
==============================================================

A car is one of the most expensive things a person ever buys, and it sits
parked most of the time. Ownership is all-or-nothing: you can't buy a slice of
a car you like, and you can't earn from a car you don't fully own. The rent a
vehicle could generate goes entirely to whoever holds the title, and there's no
simple, compliant way to split that asset — and its income — across several
people.

Carrentic turns a real car into shares. The car becomes an ERC-3643 security
token on Hedera: people buy a share, the car is rented out, and the rent flows
back to every owner on-chain, pro-rata to what they hold. An AI fleet manager
watches the car, explains how it's performing, and drafts the on-chain actions —
paying out this month's rent, marking the car's value — while the car's worth
and the rent owners receive are priced against a live market through the Uniswap
stack rather than a hard-coded number. Tokenizing a car is a single click: no
wallet, the platform signs as the issuer the way a real securities issuer does,
and every step settles on Hedera testnet with a verifiable receipt on HashScan.

You can watch the whole loop end to end. Tokenize a car and one click deploys a
real ERC-3643 token and mints its 1,000 ownership shares to the treasury. Open
the fleet to see that live token, its co-owners and their pro-rata positions,
and the car's value marked through Uniswap. Then ask the AI fleet manager to pay
out the rent, and it drafts the on-chain payout for the owners. Every figure on
the dashboard — the car's worth, the treasury, the rent — is read live, and the
tokenization itself is a genuine on-chain transaction you can open on HashScan.


==============================================================
4. HOW IT'S MADE  (tech + sponsor integrations)
==============================================================

Carrentic is one pnpm monorepo (turbo), with a Next.js app in apps/web and the
chain, pricing, and agent logic split into packages (hedera, uniswap, agent,
fund). Data flows one way: a car is tokenized on Hedera, its value and rent are
priced through Uniswap, and an OpenAI agent reasons over the result and drafts
the next on-chain action. The 3D fleet is react-three-fiber with real GLTF car
models, and the whole app deploys as a single project on Vercel.

Hedera is the core, and roughly eighty percent of the build. A car is issued as
an ERC-3643 security token through the Asset Tokenization Studio (ATS) — the
same factory/resolver ATS uses — and the entire deploy-and-mint chain runs
server-side, operator-signed, over the Hashio JSON-RPC relay with ethers v6:
deploy the token, grant the KYC / issuer / SSI-manager roles, register the
issuer, grant KYC to the treasury, and issue the 1,000 shares. There is no
wallet in the flow — the platform signs as the issuer, which is how a real
issuer actually works — and every transaction is verifiable on HashScan
(example car token: 0.0.10478278 on testnet).

Uniswap is the pricing layer. The car's NAV and the rent owners are paid are
computed through the Uniswap Trading API (computeNav, priceUsd, and a
quoteRentPayout that quotes a WETH-to-USDC rent swap), so the value on the
dashboard and the rent a co-owner receives are marked to live market prices
instead of a fixed figure. Treasury holdings are valued the same way, live per
request.

OpenAI is the fleet manager. A LangChain agent (gpt-4o-mini, behind a
provider-agnostic model layer) sits beside the dashboard: it reads the car's
position, answers questions about value, owners and rent, and drafts the on-
chain actions — the rent payout, the valuation — for the owner to approve.

Two things fought back along the way. The ATS deploy kept reverting with no logs
until we stopped feeding it Hedera "long-zero" addresses (toSolidityAddress) and
switched to the canonical EVM addresses from the mirror node — after that the
deploy and mint went through on the first try; and the factory also rejects an
ISIN with a bad check digit (WrongISINChecksum), so the demo uses a
checksum-valid ISIN. We also pivoted the tokenization off the browser
MetaMask/WalletConnect ATS SDK — which needs a human to sign every step — to the
server-side operator-signed flow above, which is what makes "tokenize a car" one
click. Finally, react-three-fiber wouldn't render inside the app's async-streamed
page (the Canvas got starved of its render loop), so the 3D car runs in an
isolated /showcase route embedded as a transparent iframe.

Across the sponsors it is one product. For Hedera (Tokenization of Anything), a
real-world asset — a car — becomes a compliant ERC-3643 token via ATS, deployed
and minted server-side and verifiable on HashScan. For Uniswap (Best Uniswap
Stack Contribution), the car's value and the rent paid to owners are priced live
through the Uniswap Trading API, with developer notes in FEEDBACK.md. And OpenAI
is the fleet manager that reads the car and drafts the on-chain actions owners
act on.

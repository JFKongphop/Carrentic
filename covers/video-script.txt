Carrentic — demo video script (short / slow pace)
Part 1: deck ≤ 1:00.   Part 2: live app ≤ 3:00.
SAY = say it slowly.  DO = screen action.  Pause where it says (pause).


================================================================
PART 1 — THE DECK  (≤ 1:00)
================================================================
Fullscreen the deck. One line per slide. Advance with → .

SLIDE 1 · Title
  SAY: "This is Carrentic. Own a share of a real car — and earn its rent."

SLIDE 2 · The asset
  SAY: "It starts with a real car. A real asset, worth real money."

SLIDE 3 · 01 Tokenize
  SAY: "First, we tokenize it — an ERC-3643 token on Hedera. One click."

SLIDE 4 · 02 Own a share
  SAY: "People buy shares. KYC-gated owners, enforced by the token."

SLIDE 5 · 03 Earn its rent
  SAY: "The car is rented. The rent goes back to owners, on-chain. Priced by Uniswap."

SLIDE 6 · Live
  SAY: "And it's live on Hedera."

SLIDE 7 · Close
  SAY: "Let me show you."


================================================================
PART 2 — THE LIVE APP  (≤ 3:00)
================================================================

LANDING  (/)
  DO:  landing page.
  SAY: "This is Carrentic — own the car, earn its rent."
  DO:  click "Enter the fleet".

TOKENIZE  (/issue)
  DO:  click "Tokenize a car". The form is already filled.
  SAY: "One click tokenizes a real car on Hedera. No wallet."
  DO:  click "Tokenize car".
  SAY: "It's deploying the token and minting the shares." (pause — let it run)
  DO:  on success, click the CAR TOKEN link → HashScan.
  SAY: "There it is on HashScan. A real token on Hedera." (close tab)

FLEET  (/fleet)
  DO:  sidebar → Fleet.
  SAY: "Here's the car. Its value is priced live by Uniswap." (pause)
  SAY: "A thousand shares. Three owners."

OWNERS & RENT  (/accounts)
  DO:  sidebar → Owners & rent.
  SAY: "The three co-owners, and their share of the rent." (pause)

AI MANAGER  (chat)
  DO:  type in the chat: "pay out this month's rent".
  SAY: "And the AI manager pays out the rent for me." (pause — let it answer)

CLOSE
  SAY: "A real car, tokenized on Hedera. Rent paid on-chain. That's Carrentic."


----------------------------------------------------------------
TIPS
----------------------------------------------------------------
- Run /issue once before recording so the second deploy is faster.
- It's fine to stay silent while the tokenize runs and while pages load —
  the screen carries it. Speak only the short lines above.
